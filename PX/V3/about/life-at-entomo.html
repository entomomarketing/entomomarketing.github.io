our origin

named after nature's smallest engineers

entomo is rooted in entomology — the study of insects. it sounds unusual for an enterprise software company, but it's deliberate. insects build colonies, weather storms, and adapt to nearly every climate on earth. they win through teamwork, resilience, and persistence — not size.

that's the company we set out to build. small teams that ship like swarms. work that compounds. an obsession with adapting faster than the world changes around us. those instincts shape how we hire, how we design, and how we treat each other.

how we work

six values, borrowed from the colony

our values aren't a poster on the wall. they're how we make decisions when no one is watching — about products, customers, and each other.

swarm, don't silo

great work happens when small teams move together. we share context, ship fast, and trust each other to fill the gaps.

adapt or evolve

tomorrow's enterprise looks nothing like yesterday's. we treat change as the default, not the exception. curiosity over comfort.

customer obsession

30M+ users count on us every day. we don't ship for ourselves — we ship for the CHRO, the manager, the new hire on day one.

resilience over heroics

insects don't burn out — they pace. we build for the long arc, protect each other's energy, and reward steady excellence.

HR-first thinking

we're HR experts who built tech, not the other way around. functional depth shapes every decision — from architecture to icon choices.

build with agency

we ship agentic software because we live agentic. take the call. make the choice. own the outcome. no permission slips required.

what makes us, us

four things that show up everywhere

you'll feel these in your first standup, your first review, your first customer call. they're the invisible architecture of how entomo runs.

01

HR-first, tech-deep

we don't bolt HR onto AI. we start with the workforce problem — the messy, human, real one — and build the platform around it. our PMs sit with CHROs. our engineers learn the talent stack. functional fluency is table stakes here.

02

agentic by instinct

we use AI agents the way we build them — to handle the routine so humans can focus on judgment. that mindset shows up in our tools, our workflows, and how we run meetings. less coordination overhead. more high-leverage work.

03

distributed, not detached

we work across continents — Singapore, Bangalore, Dubai, beyond. async-first by design, but never alone. weekly syncs, quarterly meetups, and a culture that says "drop a message, get a reply" within hours, not days.

04

customer obsessed, outcome biased

Forrester TEI doesn't validate vibes. it validates outcomes. that's the bar. we measure what we ship by what it delivers — for the CHRO, the manager, the employee. shipping fast is good. shipping useful is the point.
