responsible by design

AI your risk team can say yes to

HR is the most regulated place to hire an AI co-worker. entomo.ai is designed for that reality, not retrofitted to it.

a human owns every big call
Nothing that touches pay, rating, or employment happens without a named human approver.
every output shows its working
Citations and data lineage on what the intelligence used to answer, on every output.
your data stays yours
