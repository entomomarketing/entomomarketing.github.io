the questions assessment programs live or die on

will employees actually take them?

This is the failure mode of most programs, which is why the experience is gamified, mobile, and role-relevant. When an assessment reads like your actual job rather than a generic quiz, completion follows. One enterprise rollout reached 95% adoption.

what happens to the results?

They go to work. Each gap routes into an individual development plan with learning attached; aggregates surface as talent hotspots and nine-box views for leaders. The assessment is the beginning of a development loop, not an annual filing exercise.

who sees an individual's scores?

Access is role-governed down to the individual, on a platform certified to ISO 27001, ISO 27701, and SOC 2. Employees see their own profile and growth path; leaders see the aggregates they govern.

can this validate our competency framework?

Yes, and it should. Assessments test the framework against reality: where measured proficiency and the framework disagree, you learn something about both. Run together, they give you an architecture backed by evidence. see competency framework creation →
