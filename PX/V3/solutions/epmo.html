the problem

strategy and HR were never integrated

An EPMO exists to fix a structural problem. Most enterprises still run performance the way it was designed decades ago: horizontally, in fragments, on disparate data.

strategy and HR run on different clocks

The strategy group plans on one cadence, HR reviews on another. The cycles never meet, so the corporate scorecard and the appraisal form end up describing two different companies.

nobody has capacity to challenge KPIs

KPIs roll over year after year without scrutiny. Definitions drift, measures lose relevance and comparability, and no one owns the question of whether a target still serves the strategy.

individuals win while the business misses

People score high in reviews while their units miss targets. When ratings and results disagree, performance management has stopped measuring performance.

how it works

how the office comes to life

entomo delivers the EPMO as advisory plus platform: consultants build the office, the platform operationalizes it, and agents keep it running between reviews.

establish the office
entomo consultants assess and select your EPMO, then write its charter: the structure and governance that give it authority between strategy and HR.
→
The charter defines how the office challenges KPIs, sets one performance cadence, and personalizes incentives, the capacity most enterprises never built.
audit and cascade KPIs
A KPI audit raises measure quality, then scorecards cascade from strategy map to unit to individual. GoalForge validates every goal before the cycle begins.
→
The audit checks relevancy, consistency, comparability, reliability and completeness, and aligns measures vertically and horizontally so intelligence rests on clean data.
build the mindset
Company-wide goal setting goes live with the platform rollout, while change management socializes company goals so people are ready, willing and able to change.
→
Organization network analytics identifies the employees with real influence, how work actually gets done, and enables them as grass-roots champions of the change.
coach and reinforce
Managers and the EPMO train as performance coaches. ConvoCraft structures the conversations, and nudges reinforce the behaviors the new culture needs.
→
Reinforcement follows the experiences-beliefs-actions-results model: storytelling with data, focused feedback, gamification and incentives that make new habits stick.
sustain with intelligence
Pulse360 senses performance risk, TeamInsight diagnoses team patterns, and Insight360 reads perception gaps, so the office runs on intelligence, not opinion.
The sustain phase adds management scorecards, operational and workforce intelligence, and performance gamification so the culture outlives the rollout.

questions, answered

what enterprise teams ask us.

what exactly is an EPMO?

An enterprise performance management office is an agile structure that sits between the strategy group and human capital management. It exists to ensure continuous KPI alignment, personalized incentives and workforce intelligence, the capacity to challenge KPIs that most enterprises lack. entomo helps you establish, equip and train yours.

does entomo run the EPMO for us?

No. The office is yours. entomo assesses and selects the office, writes the charter, audits your KPIs, and trains both managers and the EPMO team as performance coaches. The platform and its agents then support the office day to day, but governance authority stays with your people.

does it replace our HCM or HRIS?

No. entomo integrates with Core HR, compensation and benefits, ERP, CRM and other transactional systems through web services, ETL over SFTP and direct protocols such as OData. Your systems of record stay where they are; entomo adds the alignment and intelligence layer on top.

what do agents automate, and what do humans approve?

Agents generate and validate goals, cascade KPIs to scorecards, surface insights, structure conversations and send nudges. Humans keep the decisions: managers and the EPMO approve scorecards, calibration runs through multi-stage moderation, and review outcomes are made by people, informed by the intelligence.

how is our data protected?

The platform is ISO 27001 and ISO 27701 certified and GDPR compliant. Your performance data powers your own intelligence, insights, nudges and scorecards inside your environment. Specific data residency requirements are addressed during scoping.

how long does implementation take?

It depends on scope, and the EPMO development journey is deliberately phased: establish the office, build the performance mindset, reinforce practices, then sustain the culture. Value arrives early in that journey; one public listed bank reached 91% adoption across 5,000+ employees within 2 months of rollout.
