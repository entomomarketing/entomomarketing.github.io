how it works

how data becomes action

one pipeline runs the whole journey: data processing, presentation, consumption, call to act, action.

connect your data
Sync performance, skill, engagement, and operational data from your existing systems into a single source of truth.
→
Data flows in through governed extraction from your central systems; one global pharmaceutical client runs SAP as the single data source behind entomo's analytics.
strata adds context
entomo strata links roles, tasks, KPIs, and skills into one connected model, benchmarked against a live market intelligence graph.
→
The graph spans 80M+ resumes, 12M job descriptions, and 40,000+ skills across 20 industry domains, refreshed monthly, benchmarking you against comparators, best-in-class, and industry.
agents analyze the signals
Pulse360 reads sentiment, TeamInsight diagnoses team health, and Insight360 flags perception gaps while ML runs descriptive to prescriptive analysis.
→
Machine learning covers all four analytics altitudes: descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive, including predictive models like attrition risk built on your own workforce data.
ask, and entomo.ai answers
Ask any workforce question in plain language; entomo.ai and DIA query, analyze, and report, from a quick chart to a full human capital report.
→
DIA is the conversational layer on top of the analytics engine: agents collaborate to assemble the answer, including reporting aligned to the ISO 30414 human capital standard.
nudges close the loop
The nudge engine pushes personalized recommendations to the person who can act, then tracks action-completion so insight becomes results.
Nudges are contextual, personalized, and push-based, automating the prescriptive step; completion tracking verifies the analytics are changing behaviour, not just informing it.

questions, answered

what enterprise teams ask us.

how long does implementation take?

It scales with scope: the number of data sources, the depth of customization, and the advisory work involved. As a reference point, one global pharmaceutical client onboarded 10,000 employees in a single day and reported 60% time saved compared with previous implementations. Your entomo team scopes a realistic timeline before you commit.

does entomo replace our HCM or BI platform?

No. entomo positions its augmented analytics as an extension beyond BI, layered over the systems you already run. It syncs workforce data from your central systems into a single source of truth; one pharmaceutical client uses SAP as the single data source behind the platform. Your HCM stays the system of record, entomo makes its data decision-ready.

what do the agents automate, and what do people approve?

Agents automate the analytical work: querying data, connecting signals across performance, skills, and engagement, generating reports, and pushing recommendations. Decisions stay with people. Nudges arrive as recommendations to the accountable owner, and the platform tracks whether action was taken. The judgment call remains yours.

how is our workforce data secured?

entomo is certified at the platform level for ISO 27001, ISO 27701, and SOC 2. Within the analytics layer, data access is customizable down to the individual, so each role sees only what it should. Data reaches the platform through governed extraction from your central systems, defined with your IT team during implementation.

can entomo produce ISO 30414 human capital reports?

Yes. The platform provides ISO 30414-compliant human capital reporting and insights with Comparator intelligence, so the same governed data behind daily decisions can support standards-aligned disclosure and board reporting. Ask entomo.ai for a report and the agent team assembles it from your live workforce data.

do we need a team of analysts to run it?

The platform is built for consumption without a query layer between leaders and answers: conversational Q&A through DIA, automated hyper-personalized dashboards, embedded reports, and self-service extraction with Excel export. Where you want deeper in-house capability, entomo's advisory team develops it as part of the engagement.
