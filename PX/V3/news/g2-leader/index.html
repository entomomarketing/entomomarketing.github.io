november 19, 2024 award

entomo recognized as a G2 leader in performance management software

top marks across ease of use, quality of support, and meets requirements, driven by feedback from enterprise customers in 50+ countries.

entomo has been recognised as a leader in performance management software on G2, the software review platform where ratings come from verified users rather than analysts.

the placement reflects scores across ease of use, quality of support and meets requirements, drawn from reviews by enterprise customers operating in more than 50 countries.

G2 rankings are recalculated continuously from verified user reviews, which makes them a useful counterweight to vendor claims: they measure what customers report after deployment rather than what was promised before it.
