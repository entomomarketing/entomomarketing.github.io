about the community

a place to think out loud, with people who get it.

the entomo community is a global gathering of CHROs, heads of HR, talent architects, and HR practitioners using entomo to transform how their enterprises work. it's where peers go to connect, learn, and share what's working — and what isn't — in the move toward agentic HR.

no vendor pitches. no panel theatre. just real conversations between leaders solving real problems, supported by the team that builds the platform you rely on.
