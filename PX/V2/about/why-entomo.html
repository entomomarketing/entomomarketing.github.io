why entomo

it's about thriving.

the enterprise of tomorrow doesn't run on dashboards or annual reviews. it runs on agentic intelligence — built for performance, skills, and growth in the daily flow-of-work.

our origin

inspired by nature's most resilient creators

entomo takes its name from entomology — the study of insects. the modestly sized wonders of nature survive, adapt, and evolve through resilience, teamwork, and persistence.

the enterprise of tomorrow reflects this same capability: thriving, adapting, and evolving continuously in a world that never stops changing. that's the philosophy that built entomo, and the philosophy that drives every agent, every workflow, every outcome on our platform.

what entomo is

the agentic intelligence platform for performance and skills

entomo is the agentic intelligence platform for performance and skills. powering 30M+ users worldwide, entomo brings together a central agent and specialized agent teams that work in the daily flow-of-work — coaching every employee, equipping every manager, and giving leaders the workforce intelligence to make better decisions, faster.

our customers include Fortune 500 enterprises, large conglomerates, and governments embarking on enterprise, industry, and national-scale workforce transformation. entomo is recognized as a leader on G2 across multiple categories and validated by Forrester TEI for delivering measurable enterprise outcomes.
