the story

the wealth of nations is now measured in skills

Every national vision — a digital economy, localized jobs, a top-20 competitiveness ranking — comes down to one question: can your people do the work the future demands? Most governments cannot answer it with data.

skills move faster than systems

National occupational frameworks are typically rebuilt every few years. Skills shift every few months. Each year the gap widens — and education drifts further from employment.

policy flies blind without skills data

Employment, education, and localization decisions carry national consequences. Most are still made from periodic surveys and static spreadsheets — not a live picture of skills supply and demand.

programs touch thousands. infrastructure reaches millions.

One-off training schemes end when their funding ends. Talent infrastructure — standards, assessments, learning, and jobs on one platform — compounds for decades.

that infrastructure is what entomo builds — and nations across asia and the gulf are already building on it.

how we do it

one platform. four layers of national talent infrastructure

The same architecture powers every engagement. Each nation starts at the layer its agenda demands — and grows into the rest.

layer 01

frame the nation's skills

A living national skills framework, not a static document. entomo's skill knowledge graph — built from 12M+ job descriptions and 80M+ resumes, refreshed every six months — localizes occupational standards, role taxonomies, and career pathways to your economy, in your languages.

layer 02

prove every skill

Every citizen builds a verified digital skill passport in under 15 minutes, guided by AI. Auto-generated, proctored assessments and digital credentials turn self-claimed skills into validated national capability.

layer 03

grow every citizen

Personalized learning paths, AI career coaching, and skill-based job matching move the workforce toward the roles the economy needs next. Learning connects directly to employment — one learner-to-earner journey on a single platform.

layer 04

steer with intelligence

National dashboards turn live skills data into a strategic cockpit: workforce readiness scores, localization metrics, supply-demand heatmaps. Policy backed by evidence, not estimates.

national career agility · with NTUC LearningHub

the career agility movement singapore's workforce runs on

NTUC LearningHub is Singapore's leading training services provider — the learning arm of the national labour movement, focused on improving every Singaporean's employability and growth through relentless economic transformation.

It set out to build a platform Singapore's workforce could use to upskill, reskill, and become tomorrow-ready. entomo powers the answer: the Career Agility Hub, where every worker builds a skills profile, sees their gaps against where the economy is heading, and receives personalized pathways, learning recommendations, and job opportunities — connected to Singapore's employers and the government job bank. Career agility became something a worker does every day, not a campaign they hear about once a year.

national digital workforce hub · republic of the philippines

one hub for the philippines' learner-to-earner journey

The Philippines' national department for information and communications technology drives the country's digital transformation — including the digital skills of its people. Its mandate: reskill and upskill professionals, students, and workers nationwide for the jobs of a digital economy.

entomo powers the country's digital workforce hub — more than a learning platform: AI talent profiling and skill-gap analysis, learning pathways mapped to national competency frameworks, auto-generated assessments and certifications, and an employer interface that turns learning into employment. Leadership gets country-wide visibility: live workforce insights, national labor trends, and future-skills forecasting for policy.

om oman
national skills framework · vision 2040
national skills & employment platform · sultanate of oman

architecting the skills backbone of vision 2040

Oman's national labour ministry — regulator and custodian of the sultanate's skills-based employment framework — is steering the country's transition to a knowledge economy under Vision 2040, which aims to place Oman among the world's top-20 developed nations.

entomo is the platform behind the initiative: living national occupational standards built on its skill knowledge graph, AI-generated proctored assessments in Arabic and English, personalized learning paths, and a policy cockpit tracking workforce readiness and localization of jobs. Hosted in-country — citizen data never leaves Oman. A national pilot validated the platform ahead of sector-by-sector scale-up, making skills the common language of employment, training, and growth.
