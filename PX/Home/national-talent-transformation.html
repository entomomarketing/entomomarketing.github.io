for governments & national agencies

the workforce infrastructure a nation can run on

Many countries are moving toward skills-based employment models, where citizens, employers, and education providers all speak a common skills language. entomo is the digital infrastructure that makes it real — national skills frameworks, workforce analytics, employability pathways, and employer engagement at scale.

This isn't a departmental tool. It's nation-building infrastructure, already delivered with ministries and national institutions across Asia and the Gulf.

the problems every government is under pressure to solve

youth unemployment is a political crisis

High youth unemployment threatens economies and governments alike. Nations need scalable systems that connect young people to real opportunities — not just more training programmes.

the skills mismatch costs billions

Industry transforms faster than education systems can respond. The widening gap between the skills available and the skills employers demand drains economies every single year.

heavy investment keeps earning fragmented returns

Skilling programmes run in silos — separate databases, no unified citizen profile, no shared measure of success. Billions invested, with no common skills language connecting any of it.

how success used to be measured
  • number trained
  • number certified
  • budget spent
→
how governments measure it now
  • employment outcomes
  • wage improvement & career progression
  • employer satisfaction
  • skills for emerging industries
  • data-driven workforce planning

entomo doesn't replace a nation's skilling programmes — it connects them into one workforce ecosystem that delivers measurable employment outcomes.

how we do it

everything a national skills ecosystem needs, working as one system

Skills frameworks feed assessments, assessments feed credentials, credentials feed jobs — and government, employers, universities, training providers, and citizens all work from the same live picture of skills.

pillar 01

skills frameworks

National occupational standards, digitized and alive — localized to your economy, bilingual, refreshed from live market data instead of ageing as PDFs.

pillar 02

skills platform

Citizen and employer tools on one platform. Every citizen builds a verified skills profile in minutes, guided by AI; every employer reads talent in the same language.

pillar 03

assessments

Competency validation at population scale — AI-generated, proctored assessments turn self-claimed skills into proven capability.

pillar 04

credentialing

Verified digital credentials citizens own and carry — recognized across learning, employment, and every programme in between.

pillar 05

labour market intelligence

Real-time demand data drawn from millions of job signals — which skills are scarce, which are emerging, where the economy is heading.

pillar 06

employment marketplace

Skill-based matching connects citizens to jobs, gigs, and career mobility — not just today's vacancies.

pillar 07

governance analytics

Policy dashboards for ministries: workforce readiness, localization, programme outcomes — evidence under every decision.

the citizen experience

citizens get learning that actually ends in a job

Every pillar lands in one consumer-grade app: a citizen builds skills against real market demand, sees openings ranked by match, and applies with a verified profile — while every step feeds the data ministries steer by.

sg singapore
live at national scale · award-winning
national career agility · with NTUC LearningHub

the career agility movement singapore's workforce runs on

NTUC LearningHub is Singapore's leading training services provider — the learning arm of the national labour movement, focused on improving every Singaporean's employability and growth through relentless economic transformation.

It set out to build a platform Singapore's workforce could use to upskill, reskill, and become tomorrow-ready. entomo powers the answer: the Career Agility Hub, where every worker builds a skills profile, sees their gaps against where the economy is heading, and receives personalized pathways, learning recommendations, and job opportunities — connected to Singapore's employers and the government job bank. Career agility became something a worker does every day, not a campaign they hear about once a year.

architecting the skills backbone of vision 2040

Oman's national labour ministry — regulator and custodian of the sultanate's skills-based employment framework — is steering the country's transition to a knowledge economy under Vision 2040, which aims to place Oman among the world's top-20 developed nations.

entomo is the platform behind the initiative: living national occupational standards built on its skill knowledge graph, AI-generated proctored assessments in Arabic and English, personalized learning paths, and a policy cockpit tracking workforce readiness and localization of jobs. for universities

from career counselling to career intelligence

Universities have invested heavily in learning delivery. But employers hire on demonstrated skills and readiness — not credentials alone. entomo connects skills profiling, market demand insight, personalised development pathways, and employer engagement into one integrated ecosystem.

It amplifies the infrastructure you already own — it doesn't replace it.

the pressure on institutions

the problems universities can't ignore

Students, employers, accreditation bodies, and government stakeholders all demand the same thing now: measurable employability outcomes.

placement rates are public and unforgiving

Graduate employment metrics are tied to reputation, rankings, and funding. When they slip, everything slips with them.

curricula drift while the market moves

Programme reviews happen every few years, but employer demand shifts every few months — and without live market insight, graduates land misaligned.

career services runs on anecdote, not data

There is no systematic way to track, measure, or improve employability at scale — and most institutions lose touch with alumni the day they graduate.
