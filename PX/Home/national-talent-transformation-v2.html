the workforce infrastructure a nation can run on

Every nation's workforce runs on skills, but the frameworks that define them freeze the day they're published. TMRW builds a living skills framework for your economy, specific to each domain and each country, then turns it into the infrastructure a nation runs on: assessments, credentials, jobs, and workforce intelligence, all speaking one language.

TMRW isn't just a learning platform, a job board, or an assessment tool. It isn't a point solution that answers one slice. It's the connective layer that brings them together, already delivered with ministries and national institutions globally.

TMRW doesn't replace a nation's skilling programmes, but it connects them into one workforce ecosystem that delivers measurable employment outcomes.

how we do it

everything a national skills ecosystem needs, working as one system

A learning platform, a job board, an assessment engine — each solves one layer. TMRW connects all seven on a single contextual skills framework, so government, employers, universities, training providers, and citizens finally work from one live picture of skills.

01

contextual skills framework

The brain of the platform — occupational standards built for each domain and each country, not a one-size template. Where standard frameworks freeze the day they publish, this one reads live demand and refreshes continuously.

02

skills platform

Citizen and employer tools on one platform. Every citizen builds a verified skills profile in minutes, guided by AI; every employer reads talent in the same language.

03

assessments

Competency validation at population scale — AI-generated, proctored assessments turn self-claimed skills into proven capability.

04

credentialing

Verified digital credentials citizens own and carry — recognized across learning, employment, and every programme in between.

05

labour market intelligence

Real-time demand data drawn from millions of job signals — which skills are scarce, which are emerging, where the economy is heading.

06

employment marketplace

Skill-based matching connects citizens to jobs, gigs, and career mobility — not just today's vacancies.

07

governance analytics

Policy dashboards for ministries: workforce readiness, localization, programme outcomes — evidence under every decision.

national career agility · with NTUC LearningHub

the career agility movement Singapore’s workforce runs on

NTUC LearningHub is Singapore's leading training services provider — the learning arm of the national labour movement, focused on improving every Singaporean's employability and growth through relentless economic transformation.

It set out to build a platform Singapore's workforce could use to upskill, reskill, and become tomorrow-ready. TMRW powers the answer: the Career Agility Hub, where every worker builds a skills profile, sees their gaps against where the economy is heading, and receives personalized pathways, learning recommendations, and job opportunities — connected to Singapore's employers and the government job bank. Career agility became something a worker does every day, not a campaign they hear about once a year.

om Oman
national skills framework · Vision 2040
national occupational standards · with the Ministry of Labour

turning national skills standards into careers citizens can build

Oman’s Ministry of Labour, regulator and custodian of the sultanate’s skills-based employment framework, is steering the country’s transition to a knowledge economy under Vision 2040, which aims to place Oman among the world’s top-20 developed nations.

In partnership with Muscat Technology Investments (MTI), TMRW is enabling the initiative with living national occupational standards built on its proprietary KSAT Framework, AI-generated proctored assessments, personalized learning paths, and an intelligence cockpit surfacing future trends and skill demand.

It is building the complete National Occupational Standards for all of Oman’s priority sectors, and executing them on a state-of-the-art platform that carries individuals across their career journey.
